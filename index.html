The good parts of the pro players. Then more.

📁

Folders, your way

Add a folder and Vivora keeps its sub-folders. Make your own folders (“Telugu movies”, “Kids”) and drag videos into them.

🎞️

Plays almost anything

MP4, MKV, WebM, MOV, MP3, FLAC, AAC, Opus, WAV… The desktop app converts AVI, WMV, FLV and AC3/DTS sound on the fly.

💬

Subtitles & audio tracks

Load .srt, .vtt or .ass, or pick embedded ones. Shift timing, size and colour. Switch the audio language when a movie has several.

🪟

Full screen, mini, pop-out

Full screen, a draggable mini player while you browse, picture-in-picture, and an always-on-top pop-out on desktop.

👆

Gestures & shortcuts

Double-tap to skip, swipe for volume, brightness and seeking, hold for 2×. VLC-style keys on the computer.

🔁

Pro tools

A–B repeat, 0.25×–4× speed, frame-by-frame, aspect & zoom, rotate, picture filters, screenshots, sleep timer and resume where you left off.

Sound that fills the room.

A 10-band equalizer with presets (Movie, Music, Vocal, Bass boost, Night…), loudness levelling so whispers and explosions sit together, clear-voice dialogue boost, balance, and volume up to 200% with a limiter so nothing distorts.

Free music in 25 languages.

Stream songs shared under Creative Commons or in the public domain on the Internet Archive — Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, English, Spanish, Arabic, Japanese and more. Each song shows its licence and source. Your own music plays too.
